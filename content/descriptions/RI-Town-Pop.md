## Little Rhody Maps
Welcome to Little Rhody Maps! I’m Ian and this is my site, it is a place for me to talk about maps, GIS and all sorts of data that is relevant to Rhode Island. Every post will have an interactive map accompanied by a post detailing what inspired me, the data behind the map and anything else I find relevant. 

I was drawn to making this site from following the [Strong Towns](https://www.strongtowns.org/) movement closely. The Strong Towns book by Charles L. Marohn Jr. sparked my interest in thinking more locally. I highly recommend this book for anyone interested in urban development or just looking for a new perspective on our towns. 

I have since become fascinated by all the ways in which our towns work and the ways data can give us insight into our local lives. I plan to use this site as a way for me to explore those curiosities while honing my mapping and GIS skills. These posts will document my learning journey and be a way to share those learnings with the world.


## About the Data
The data used to create this map comes from The American Community Survey (ACS). The ACS is an ongoing U.S. Census Bureau project that releases yearly data on American life. It offers both 1-year and 5-year reports, with the 5-year version aggregating larger amounts of data for more accurate estimates. The data on this map comes directly from the 2024 5-year ACS.
